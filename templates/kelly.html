How to Use This

Full Kelly maximizes long-term growth but leads to large swings. Only use if your probability estimates are very precise.

Half Kelly (recommended) reduces variance by 50% while retaining ~75% of the growth rate. Good balance for most bettors.

Quarter Kelly is the most conservative. Use when uncertain about your edge.
